Roci is a personal planning tool I'm building for my own use. It runs locally on my Mac, reads my Google Calendar to see when I'm busy, breaks big projects into small steps, and schedules those steps into open time as calendar blocks. It also tracks the AI agents working on my projects and books time for me to review their work.

Roci is not offered to the public.
